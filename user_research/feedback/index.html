    Findings

    The following was the feedback recorded after the questions were asked . I interviewed different family members and recorded their answers based on different questions asked.

    Challenges during the interview process

    • Some family members epecially the (old members) had difficulties understanding the tech related questions but I had to take extra time to explain some of the questions to them.

    tip

    Despite some members having challenges answering some questions , The interview process was seamles and it gave me great insight to build the system to solve the challenges the members are facing.

    Different feedback from different members

    Feature requests & convenience

    A younger member (20s, tech-comfortable) felt the payment process should be automated so that they can pay from the comfort of their own phone, rather than needing to hand over cash or make a manual transfer and then separately report it. They also wanted the system to automatically update their payment record and send a receipt confirming the payment went through, so they're not left wondering whether it registered.

    Quote

    "The family system should ease my payment process by either prompting me to make the payment."

    — Younger member, 20s, tech-comfortable

    Reducing manual calculation errors

    A Treasurer-adjacent member in their 60s felt the system should make payment records automatic and handle the money calculations itself, rather than leaving that to memory. Tracking payments manually — adding up who's paid, who hasn't, what's left — was tiring to keep up with, and compiling the records by hand left room for mistakes.

    Quote

    "The system should help with the money calculations."

    — Treasurer-adjacent member, 60s

    Automated reporting & reducing paperwork

    The Secretary said the system should produce the annual report in time for the December AGM meeting on its own — compiling all the year's records by hand takes time, and by the end of that process it's easy to get tired and leave some details out. They wanted the system to surface feedback/summaries on every campaign as it closes, not just once a year, and to generate the full annual report automatically. They also pointed out that members should be able to download meeting minutes straight to their phone, removing the need to print and distribute hardcopy minutes, which is a real, recurring cost.

    Reminders & notifications

    A member in their 30s said the system should send them reminders — for upcoming events, meetings, and any campaign that needs money — because work keeps their mind busy and it's easy to lose track of family matters in the meantime. For them, reminders aren't a nice-to-have; they're what keeps them able to stay involved in family events at all.

    Quote

    "I want to receive reminders and notification on my phone of any upcoming event or function."

    — Member, 30s

    Insight

    Success

    The system's real job, based on this research, isn't just digitizing records — it's removing the family's collective reliance on individual memory.
