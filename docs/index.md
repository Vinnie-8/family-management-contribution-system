
# User Research Analysis
!!! info
    This is the **FAMILY CONTRIBUTION MANAGEMENT SYSTEM** whereby the vision is to digitize how families manage meetings, contributions, campaigns, events, and payments

Before any feature was designed, research was conducted with family members across different age groups — young and old — to understand how the family currently manages contributions, what's frustrating about it, and what a better system would actually need to do for them.

!!! note
    "Methodology" Research was conducted through one-on-one interviews. Questions were posed directly to each interviewee, and their responses were recorded for analysis.

### Research Questions
Before any schema or endpoint was designed, this started with talking to the people who would actually use it — the section below captures the questions that shaped everything that follows below.

!!! tip 
    "Questions were tailored by age and tech familiarity" The same underlying topics were explored with every interviewee, but phrasing and follow-up depth varied by age and tech comfort — younger family members generally have far more exposure to technology than older members, whose tech familiarity is often minimal. Adjusting how each question was asked (without changing what it was really asking) made sure older, less tech-familiar members could answer just as fully as younger ones.

### Questions

1.How would you describe the perfect family management system, in your own words?

2.Which feature would you most want included in the system, and how would that feature help with something that's currently hard to do?

3.What challenges has the manual way of handling things caused for you, and what do you expect a system to solve?

4.What does "modernizing" the family's record-keeping and payments with technology mean to you, and how do you think it would improve the way family records and payments are handled?
